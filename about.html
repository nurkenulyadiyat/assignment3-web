Games bring us together

About LEVEL UP

A small club with a simple idea: make room for good games and good company.

Our story

From a game night to a club

In our fictional story, LEVEL UP started when a group of students wanted a place to play after class. Their weekly game nights grew into the idea for a friendly gaming club.

The club brings PC and console players together, whether they are trying their first game or meeting their regular team. This website is a student project; LEVEL UP is not a real business.

Equipment for every player

Each zone has a different setup, with the same focus on comfort and easy play.

  • Standard PC: 144 Hz monitors, gaming keyboards, mice, and headsets.
  • VIP PC: 240 Hz monitors, mechanical keyboards, headsets, and extra desk space.
  • Console Zone: a large TV, a sofa, and two wireless controllers.

Keep it friendly

House rules

A few simple rules help everyone enjoy their session.

  1. Respect other players and the staff.
  2. Take care of the equipment.
  3. Keep food and drinks away from keyboards and consoles.
  4. Play fairly. Cheating and harassment are not welcome.
  5. Keep the sound at a comfortable level.
  6. Sign out of your gaming accounts when you finish.
